Fee payment terms
01
Fee calculation frequency
P&L is calculated annually at the end of the 1-year lock-in. No mid-year fee is charged.
02
Fee basis
All fee calculations are on gross (pre-tax) profit. Tax filing and payment is entirely the client's own responsibility.
03
Fee payment timeline
Client transfers manager fee within 7 working days of the lock-in end date, after reviewing the P&L statement provided.
04
High water mark
If the year ends in a loss, no fee is charged. Fee resumes only when capital crosses the previous peak (starting capital).
05
Early exit clause
Exit is allowed only if drawdown exceeds 25% from peak. Fee is settled on the exit date, not year-end.
06
Plan lock-in
Chosen plan (A or B) is locked for the full 1-year term. Switch is only allowed at renewal before the next term begins.
07
P&L statement
A trade-by-trade gross P&L summary is provided at year-end showing gross profit, hurdle calculation (Plan B), and net fee payable.
